## 3. Help centre: how features behave (32 articles)
- **Auto captions** ("Recognise subtitles"): Captions ▸ Auto captions ▸ choose audio source + language ▸ Generate ▸ fix in the timeline. Speaker separation, punctuation refinement and batch SRT export may be desktop/web only. "Always review."
- **Text to speech**: only on text items; select the text ▸ Text to speech ▸ voice ▸ Generate; fails on slow network or muted/out-of-range audio.
- **Lip sync**: Video ▸ Basic ▸ Lip sync; needs a clear face; rolled out by region.
- **Auto Cut**: trims and syncs clips to **music beats, speech pauses or a text prompt**; cloud; not with templates or shared projects; desktop 5.0+.
- **Transitions**: need two touching clips; drag from the Transitions tab, set length on the timeline. A fade-in is an *effect*, not a transition (users get lost here).
- **Effects not applying**: no clip selected, effect on its own layer instead of the clip, playhead outside it, stacked effects cancel. (Lesson: attach effects to clips.)
- **Chroma key**: picker, Strength, Shadow, Edge feathering (desktop) vs "Intensity" (web/mobile); problems come from uneven green, wrong colour, clothes the same colour.
- **Text not showing**: off-screen position, under another layer, opacity 0, entry animation hiding it.
- **Templates**: desktop has only a small set and no search; many templates are mobile-only or region-locked; desktop can't render some template effects ("Auto-cut to beat", "Dynamic zoom") in preview.
- **Watermark**: comes from a CapCut ending clip or template elements, not the export itself.
- **Export a part**: ⇧X then In/Out, or I and O.
- **Premiere projects**: can't be opened; export a file instead.
- **Pictures**: desktop has no batch limit, 200 MB each; JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, HEIC (Mac); no RAW.
- **Trending transitions 2026** (editorial): speed ramp, beat sync, glitch, cinematic fade & zoom, smart transitions, smooth swipe, before & after, slow motion + blur.
- Most help-centre space goes to: templates (~14 articles), installs/versions, AI features, billing, export/watermark, transitions, captions/voice.

## 4. Desktop guides (step by step)
- **Long video to shorts** (home): upload ▸ choose the part ▸ caption template ▸ length **60 s / 60–90 s / 90 s–3 min** ▸ Convert ▸ Export or Edit.
- **Split scenes**: right-click a clip ▸ Split scenes (automatic shot detection).
- **Speed**: Standard 0.1–100×; Curve presets Montage, Hero, Bullet, Jump cut, Flash in, Flash out, custom.
- **Optical flow** (smooth slow-mo): Video ▸ Basic ▸ Optical flow, 30/50/60 fps, needs a slowed clip, desktop only.
- **Auto reframe**: Video ▸ Basic ▸ Auto reframe ▸ ratio; stabilisation level and camera speed.
- **Enhance quality**: Video ▸ Basic ▸ HD / UHD.
- **Voice**: Audio ▸ Basic: Vocal isolation (keep/remove vocals), Enhance voice (strength), Loudness normalization, Noise reduction; Voice changer filters; record a voice-over from the mic button (new track).
- **Overlay / PiP**: drag a clip above the main track; Scale, position, blend modes for light leaks and grain.
- **Zoom effect**: Effects ▸ "Zoom lens" / "Optical zoom" on a clip, set strength and speed.
- **Scrolling text**: Text ▸ Animation ▸ Loop ▸ Scrolling.
- **Mirror**: toolbar button over the timeline.
- **Export**: name, place, resolution (to 4K/8K), bit rate, codec (H.264/HEVC/AV1), format MP4/MOV, frame rate, audio only (MP3/WAV/FLAC), copyright check, share to TikTok/YouTube with title, visibility and comment settings.
