_CapCut 9.7.1 (297) for Mac, hands-on 2026-11-12 with the Italian interface. English names are translations; the Italian label is in brackets when it helps find it in `screens/`. Bundle-level detail (every string, the project format, models) is in `capcut-bundle-analysis.md`._

💎 = Pro badge seen on this account (free plan). "Free" / "Free trial" badges are CapCut's own.

## 5. Free vs Pro (seen on this account, 2026-11-12)

| Free | Pro 💎 |
|---|---|
| Cut, arrange, transform, blend/opacity, canvas, mask, custom cut-out, chroma key | **Auto captions**, bilingual captions, filler-word detection |
| Standard speed, speed curves (2 uses), Flash speed effect | Smooth slow motion, most speed effects |
| LUT, HSL, curves, colour wheels, sliders | Auto adjust, colour match, colour correction |
| Text, preset text looks, text to speech (some voices) | Text/body tracking, AI avatar, many voices |
| AI expand, eye contact (free); AI remix, lip sync (trial) | Stabilize, enhance quality (3 a day), noise, optical flow, AI removal, relight, auto reframe, flickers, AI movement, camera tracking, motion blur, auto background removal |
| Some music, effects, transitions, filters, animations | Many tiles, **stock clips** in the Library |

**At export** CapCut lists every Pro item used ("Get Pro and save 21.6 € to unlock these features" › "Pro materials": the two stock clips) with *Back to editing* / *Get Pro free* / *Get the discount* (screen 31). Prices on capcut.com (EUR): Pro 29.99/month or 259.99/year; Ultra 68/month; Teams 14.99/seat; 7-day free trial (see `capcut-web-notes.md`).

## 8. Numbers worth copying
- New text: size 15 (CapCut units), centred, white; default text length ~3 s.
- Speed: 0.1–100×; pitch change off by default; curves are 6 named shapes + custom.
- Picture default length 5 s (preference); project default 30 fps.
- Captions: split with ↩, new line ⌘↩; style changes apply to all captions.
- Loudness targets offered: −23 (default), **−14 (YouTube)**, −12 (TikTok) LUFS.
- Bin keeps deleted projects 30 days.
