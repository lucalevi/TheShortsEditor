Shorts Editor {{VERSION}}: how to open it

Shorts Editor is free and isn't sold through Apple, so macOS can't check it for you
and warns you the first time. These steps tell your Mac you trust it. You do them
once for each version you download.

1. Drag "Shorts Editor" onto "Applications" in this window.

2. Open your Applications folder and double-click Shorts Editor.
   macOS says it could not verify that the app is free of malware.
   Click "Done" (not "Move to Trash").

3. Open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security.
   Next to "Shorts Editor" was blocked…, click "Open Anyway",
   then enter your Mac's password.

4. Click "Open Anyway" once more. From now on Shorts Editor opens like any other app.

Prefer Terminal? After step 1, this does the same as steps 2–4:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Shorts Editor.app"

Needs: a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26 or later.
