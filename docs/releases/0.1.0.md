The first version of Shorts Editor you can download: a Mac video editor made only for YouTube Shorts (vertical, up to 3 minutes), as simple as iMovie.

**Needs** a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later. Shorts Editor isn't signed with a paid Apple developer account, so macOS warns you the first time you open it: see **How to open.txt** in the disk image, or [Opening it the first time](https://github.com/lucalevi/TheShortsEditor#opening-it-the-first-time).

### What it does

- **Edit:** drop in videos, photos and sound; split, trim, reorder and change speed on a magnetic timeline; B-roll, split screen and picture-in-picture; freeze frames, replays, backwards clips, smooth slow motion; undo for everything.
- **Captions from your voice**, made on your Mac in English, Italian, German or French, with word-by-word styles, key words and emoji, editing by text, filler words and pauses removed in one go.
- **Titles and stickers** with animations, following a moving subject; spoken voice-overs from text.
- **Sound:** music made to fit your Short's length and mood, beats to cut to, the music lowered while people talk, voice clean-up and effects, sound effects.
- **Looks and effects:** filters, colour sliders, Enhance, steadying shaky clips, background removal and green screen, transitions.
- **One click:** styles, Magic Short, storyboards and templates, a cover picture.
- **Export** an MP4 with YouTube's recommended settings, at the right loudness, with a check that your text isn't hidden under YouTube's buttons.

Everything runs on your Mac: nothing is uploaded. Shorts Editor ▸ Check for Updates… tells you when there's a newer version.
